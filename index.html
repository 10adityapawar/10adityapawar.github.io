Pineapple Clipper

A personal tool for posting short stream highlights.

Pineapple Clipper is a small program that runs on its owner's own computer. It finds popular moments from public livestreams, turns them into short vertical videos, and uploads them to YouTube channels and TikTok accounts that belong to its owner.

It is not a public service. Nobody else signs up for it or uses it, and it only posts to the owner's own accounts.

What it uses your account for

  • YouTube: uploading videos to the channel you choose when you sign in, and reading that channel's name to confirm the sign-in worked.
  • TikTok: posting videos to the account you sign in with.
