Pineapple Clipper

A personal tool for posting short stream highlights.

Privacy PolicyTerms of Service

Pineapple Clipper is a small desktop program that runs on its owner's own computer. It finds popular moments from public livestreams, turns them into short vertical videos with captions, and lets the owner review each one before posting it to the owner's own channels.

It is not a public service. Nobody else signs up for it or uses it, and it only posts to the owner's own accounts.

Uses YouTube API Services

Pineapple Clipper uses YouTube API Services to upload videos. The owner reviews every video and sets its title, description, and visibility before it is uploaded. By using the app, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What it uses your account for

  • Uploading videos to the YouTube channel you choose when you sign in.
  • Reading that channel's name to confirm the sign-in worked.

You can remove its access at any time at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
