Privacy Policy

Pineapple Clipper · Last updated September 28, 2026

Who uses this app

Pineapple Clipper is a personal desktop tool used only by its owner to post short videos to the owner's own YouTube channels. It has no other users and is not offered to the public.

YouTube API Services

Pineapple Clipper uses YouTube API Services to upload videos. By using Pineapple Clipper, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Your use of Google and YouTube data is also covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What data it accesses

When you sign in with Google, the app asks for permission to:

  • upload videos to the YouTube channel you choose at sign-in, and
  • read that channel's basic details (its name), to confirm which channel is signed in.

It does not read your videos, comments, subscribers, analytics, or any other account data.

What it stores, and where

Everything stays on the owner's own computer. The app has no server, uses no cookies or tracking, and collects no analytics. It stores:

  • the sign-in tokens Google issues, so it can upload without asking you to sign in every time;
  • the name of each signed-in channel; and
  • the ID, title, and description of each video it uploaded, so the same clip is never posted twice.

How the data is used and shared

The data above is used only to upload videos you have reviewed and approved, and to show which channel each upload went to. It is not sold, shared with, or transferred to anyone. Data received from Google APIs is used in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

TikTok

The app does not connect to TikTok. Videos are posted to TikTok by hand, in TikTok's own website or app.

Removing access and deleting data

You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Deleting the app's secrets and data folders from the computer permanently removes all stored tokens and records.
