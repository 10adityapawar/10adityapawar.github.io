Privacy Policy

Pineapple Clipper · Last updated September 28, 2026

Who uses this app

Pineapple Clipper is a personal tool used only by its owner to post videos to the owner's own YouTube channels and TikTok accounts. It has no other users.

What data it accesses

  • YouTube (Google): permission to upload videos to the channel chosen at sign-in, and read-only access to that channel's basic details (such as its name).
  • TikTok: permission to post videos to the signed-in account and read its basic profile details.

How the data is used and stored

Sign-in tokens are saved only on the owner's own computer and are used only to upload videos and confirm which account is signed in. The app has no server and does not collect analytics.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with, or transferred to anyone else. Data received from Google APIs is used only for the purposes above and complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google) or in TikTok's app settings. Deleting the saved tokens from the computer removes all stored data.
